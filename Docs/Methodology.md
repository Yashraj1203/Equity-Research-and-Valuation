# Research Methodology

## 1. Company research
Review public annual reports, financial results, investor presentations, operating disclosures and analyst/investor materials.

## 2. Historical financials
Normalize reported revenue, EBITDA, PAT and operating volumes into a consistent research model.

## 3. Forecasting
Build FY27E–FY29E assumptions around revenue growth, EBITDA margin and PAT margin. 

## 4. Valuation
Use DCF plus peer multiples as complementary valuation frameworks.

## 5. Scenario analysis
Use Bear/Base/Bull operating assumptions and valuation multiples to show sensitivity rather than relying on a single point estimate.

## 6. Research discipline
Separate reported facts from estimates. Identify risks and monitorables. Avoid presenting model outputs as guaranteed outcomes.

## 7. Regulatory framing
SEBI's current Research Analyst framework emphasizes reliable information, defined terminology, disclosures and unbiased research. This portfolio artifact is therefore explicitly labeled educational and is not presented as a registered research report.
